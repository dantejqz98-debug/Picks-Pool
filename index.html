Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Fight Locks, pools, scoring, pricing, payments, and host/admin tools.

General Questions
What is Fight Locks?

Fight Locks is a fight-pool organizer and picks tracking platform. Hosts can create pools, invite players, set scoring rules, and track picks during fight night.

Is Fight Locks a betting site?

No. Fight Locks is not a sportsbook, betting site, gambling platform, or payment processor. Fight Locks is a pool organizer and scoring platform.

Can I use Fight Locks for free?

Yes. During launch, Fight Locks Free Pool includes the full standard Fight Locks experience with unlimited players through UFC 329.

Who is Fight Locks for?

Fight Locks is for fight fans, friend groups, coworkers, sports bars, restaurants, creators, gyms, venues, and communities that want a cleaner way to organize fight-night picks.

Do players need to pay Fight Locks to join a pool?

No. Players can join pools and make picks without paying Fight Locks. Pricing is mainly for hosts/admins who want to create larger, more customized, or recurring pools.

Pool Setup
How do I create a pool?

A host creates a pool, chooses the setup, shares the invite code or link, and players join before the fights begin.

How do people join my pool?

Players can join using the pool invite link or code shared by the host/admin.

Can I create a private pool?

Yes. Pools are designed to be private and shared with the people you invite.

Can I invite friends, coworkers, or group chats?

Yes. Fight Locks works great for friends, family, coworkers, group chats, and fight-night watch parties.

Can I run a pool for a restaurant, sports bar, venue, or community?

Yes. Venue and branded community options can be handled separately from the standard host plans when a group needs a custom fight-night experience.

Picks & Scoring
What picks can players make?

Depending on the pool settings, players may be able to pick the fight winner, method of victory, round prediction, Over/Under, Lock of the Night, and other scoring options enabled by the host/admin.

What does Fight Locks Free Pool include?

Fight Locks Free Pool includes the full standard Fight Locks experience during launch, including winner, method, round, Over/Under, Lock of the Night, Fastest Finish Tiebreaker, standard scoring, leaderboard, and pool controls.

What is method of victory?

Method of victory means predicting how a fighter wins, such as KO/TKO, submission, or decision.

What is Lock of the Night?

Lock of the Night is a player's strongest pick on the fight card. Depending on the pool settings, getting your Lock of the Night correct earns +2 points.

What is an Over/Under pick?

An Over/Under pick lets players predict whether a fight will end before or after a set point, depending on the pool's scoring rules.

Can scoring rules be customized?

Yes. Hosts can use the standard Fight Locks scoring experience on Free Pool, Event Pass, and Commissioner pools. Bigger or recurring hosting needs are handled by Fight Locks Event Pass, Fight Locks Commissioner, or All Events Pass.

Can the host change scoring rules?

Yes. Hosts/admins can manage their pool's scoring setup where supported. During launch, Free Pools include unlimited players through UFC 329.

Can scoring rules vary by pool?

Yes. Scoring may vary depending on the host/admin settings. Players should always review their pool's scoring key before making picks.

When can players see everyone's picks?

Players can see everyone's picks once the fights begin. This keeps picks private before the event starts and makes fight night more exciting once the action begins.

Does Fight Locks update the leaderboard?

Yes. Fight Locks is designed to help players follow the leaderboard, track points, and see where they stand during fight night.

Payments & Pool Money
Does Fight Locks handle pool payments?

No. Fight Locks does not accept entry fees, hold pool funds, process player payments, manage payouts, distribute winnings, or take a percentage of private pool money.

Can a pool be completely free?

Yes. A host can run a pool just for fun, bragging rights, or a free group competition.

Can hosts collect money from players privately?

If a host chooses to organize a private payment arrangement, that is handled entirely outside Fight Locks between the host and participants.

Can hosts decide their own prize format?

Yes. Hosts can decide their own private group format outside Fight Locks, such as winner-takes-all, top two, top three, or any other arrangement that works for their group.

Does Fight Locks take a percentage of pool money?

No. Fight Locks does not take any percentage of private pool money, entry amounts, prize arrangements, or payouts.

What payment methods can hosts use outside Fight Locks?

Any private payments are handled outside the platform using whatever method the group chooses, such as Cash App, Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, Apple Cash, cash, or another option.

Is Fight Locks responsible for private payment arrangements?

No. Private payment arrangements are handled entirely by the host and participants outside Fight Locks.

What does Fight Locks charge for?

Fight Locks charges for platform access and pool-hosting tools, such as larger pools, premium customization, recurring host tools, and advanced admin features.

Fastest Finish Tiebreaker

Only used if players are tied after the full card is complete. Guess the quickest finish from any fight on the entire card. Closest guess ranks higher.

Pick the round and elapsed time for the fastest finish anywhere on the card. Use elapsed time into the round, not the broadcast clock remaining. This does not add points. If players are tied after the card is over, the tied player closest to the actual fastest finish ranks higher.

Example: R1 at 0:13 means the quickest finish happens 13 seconds into Round 1. If the broadcast clock shows 4:47 left, enter 0:13.

Tiebreaker example: if two players finish with the same score, and the fastest finish is R1 at 1:50 elapsed, the player whose tiebreaker is closest to 1:50 wins the tie.
